How Teamwork reasons

Every screen and every fixed answer in the app is a node. Choose one to see which ideas it draws on and the one sentence of reasoning behind each link. Switch to Ideas to see how the ideas relate to each other. Two kinds of reasoning run through all of it: decision science (how to weigh evidence and choices) and organizational behaviour (how people and set-ups behave). A third group holds the guardrails and practices that keep the advice honest.
